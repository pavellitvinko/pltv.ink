01 / 05 · RW Pass Bot

Thousands of passengers.  One Solution.

Finding a train ticket sometimes can feel like winning the lottery. Popular routes sell out quickly, leaving passengers endlessly refreshing booking pages, hoping a seat becomes available. I built RW Pass Bot to solve this frustration – a Telegram bot that monitors ticket availability and instantly notifies travelers when seats open up. What started as a personal solution has grown into a service helping thousands of passengers get where they need to go.

Since February 2026, RW Pass Bot has spotted over 4,000 available train tickets, delivered more than 7,500 alerts, and grown to serve over 2,300 travelers a month.
